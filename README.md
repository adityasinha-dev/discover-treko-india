TREKO — ACTUAL PRODUCT CONCEPT

Treko is an India-focused travel discovery and planning platform.

Treko has only two core service categories:

1. Accommodation

Users can discover accommodation options available at their selected destination.

Examples:

Hotels

Resorts

Homestays

Guest Houses

Villas

2. Cab Travel Operators

Users can discover local/regional cab travel operators serving that destination or region.

Examples:

Local taxi operators

Outstation cab operators

Tour & travel operators

Local sightseeing cab providers

Airport/railway transfer operators

Treko does NOT currently provide flights, trains, buses, international travel, or other transportation categories.

The core user journey is:

Choose a destination in India → Explore stays → Explore cab operators → Compare options → Contact/Book through the listed provider

The purpose of Treko is to make it easier for travelers to find both their stay and local cab travel options for the same destination from one platform.
